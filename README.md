## Privacy Policy

Effective Date: *2025-11-16*

Positive Parenting (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

---

### Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve the Positive Parenting App:

1. **Account Information**: Your name, email address, and login credentials.
2. **Child Development & Behavior Data**: Notes, observations, and other behavior-related details that you choose to record about your child.

We **do not** collect device data, cookies, or other technical information, and we **do not share** any personal or child-related data with third parties.

---

### How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to deliver the app’s core features, including:

* **Tracking and Visualization**: Processing your notes and observations to help you track and visualize your child’s progress over time.
* **Account Management**: Creating and maintaining your account and sending essential service communications (e.g., password resets or important updates).

We do **not** use your information for advertising or any other unrelated purposes.

---

### Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secure cloud database.
To protect it, we implement industry-standard safeguards:

* **Password Protection**: All account passwords are securely hashed before storage.
* **Encrypted Connections**: Data exchanged between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
* **Access Control**: Authorization procedures ensure that only you (and anyone you explicitly share your account with) can access your information.

Although we take strong measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password for added protection.

---

### Your Rights and Choices

You remain in control of your information. At any time, you may:

* **Access and Export**: Generate and download a complete copy of all data we store about you and your child directly through the app.
* **Delete Your Account**: Permanently delete your account and all associated data. When you delete your account, all personal and child-related information is removed from our systems and cannot be recovered.

---

### Children’s Privacy

The Positive Parenting App is intended for use by parents and guardians.
We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All child-related information is entered by the parent or guardian and remains under their control.

---

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or legal requirements.
If we make significant changes, we will post the updated policy within the app so you can review it before it takes effect.

---

### Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at [support@positiveparenting.dk](mailto:support@positiveparenting.dk)
