## In-App Purchase Terms

### Subscription Plans

- Monthly: €2.50/month
- Semi-annual: €12/6 months (€2/month, save €6)
- Annual: €16/year (€1.33/month, save €14)

### Free Trial

- 14-day free trial available for all plans.
- Trial automatically converts to paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends.

### Benefits of Paid Plans

- Add behaviors to focus on what matters most for your child.
- Get AI-powered goal ideas for both you and your child to guide positive behavior in everyday moments.
- Share child profiles easily with co-parents or caregivers for smooth teamwork.
- Track behaviors in multiple ways and see your child's progress over time with clear bar charts.

### Billing & Renewal

- Subscriptions billed via Stripe and automatically renew.
- Cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the billing period.
- No prorated refunds for partial periods.

### Requirements

- Must be 18 or older to subscribe.
- Subscription requires acceptance of our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/xbisa/privacy-policy/blob/main/README.md)
